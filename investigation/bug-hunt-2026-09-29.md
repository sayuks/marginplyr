# Repository-wide public-seam bug hunt

Investigated: 2026-09-29
Baseline: `a9313501cd73eabd84415bc2cf0387e69e55f71e`

## Outcome

Four independently repairable SQL defects were reproduced and filed:

- [#725](https://github.com/sayuks/marginplyr/issues/725): a public `.env` output fails when generated Margin SQL reads it. The minimum is a one-set summary with `.id = ".env"`; a sorted ordinary `.env` summary output, a multi-set expansion identifier, and a contextual share reading an ordinary `.env` summary are further routes. Ordinary DuckDB `mutate()` accepts the output name, and the local Margin calls work.
- [#726](https://github.com/sayuks/marginplyr/issues/726): a `.env` input column fails as a DuckDB Margin summary's fixed key or Grouping dimension, even in a one-set plan with a missing Margin label. Expansion and `inspect_grouping()` accept the dimension. Direct dbplyr grouping also fails, so the Margin summary needs an identifier-safe construction path as the SQLite repair in #722 did.
- [#728](https://github.com/sayuks/marginplyr/issues/728): a `.env` payload column fails in a multi-set SQLite or DuckDB expansion. A one-set expansion, ordinary backend projection, and local, dtplyr, and Arrow multi-set controls pass.
- [#729](https://github.com/sayuks/marginplyr/issues/729): SQLite rollup summaries fail when an eligible ordinary `.env` summary feeds a Parent or Total share, or when the share output itself is named `.env`. One-set controls pass. DuckDB's share-source route belongs to #725; its share-output route passes.

The issues contain executable minimal reproductions, controls, observed diagnostics, and acceptance criteria. The SQL cases were run against live in-memory SQLite and DuckDB connections. The SQLite analogues in #721 and #722 were closed before this investigation and passed the corresponding live controls on this baseline. No package or test-suite source was changed during the hunt.

## Search and controls

The clean baseline's full `pkgload::load_all()` plus `testthat::test_dir()` run ended with 20,502 passes, zero failures, zero warnings, and 13 CRAN-context snapshot skips. Temporary deterministic probes then compared public results with independent calculations:

| Probe | Executed range | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct additive summary | 20 seeds × local, dtplyr, SQLite, DuckDB, Arrow × rollup, cube, explicit grouping sets; 0–8 rows, fixed and variable missing keys | 300 comparisons matched independently grouped totals and counts. |
| Parent and Total shares | 30 seeds × local, dtplyr, SQLite, DuckDB; positive measures, duplicate rows, missing fixed and variable keys | 120 backend cases matched rowwise numerator and denominator calculations. |
| Expansion | 30 seeds × five inputs; repeated grouping-set occurrences kept | 150 backend cases matched independently expanded source-row multisets. |
| Type and order | Eight key types × five inputs × summary/expansion; separately 20 seeds × five inputs × first/last × summary/expansion | No unexpected type, row-count, or Margin-order difference was found in 80 type checks and 200 order checks. |
| Three-dimensional cube | 15 seeds × five inputs | All 75 cases matched the eight-subset plan, additive totals, Grouping bits, and Grouping identifiers. |
| Nesting | 25 seeds × local/dtplyr × two verbs × both `.keep` values | All 200 cases matched independently selected source-row cells and outer keys. |
| Display labels | 20 seeds × five inputs × three grouping specifications; source values deliberately collided with per-dimension labels and `.check_margin_label = FALSE` | All 300 results matched the independent additive oracle when occurrence identifiers distinguished rows. |

The initial special-name sweep crossed `.env`, `.data`, `.by`, `.groups`, dtplyr-style names, SQL keywords, nonsyntactic names, and an internal-looking name over live SQLite and DuckDB results. `.by` and `.groups` used as summary outputs were excluded because the package deliberately refuses them on local input too. That sweep found the DuckDB `.env` routes in #725 and #726.

A follow-up crossed 12 `.env` roles with local, dtplyr, SQLite, DuckDB, and Arrow inputs: fixed key, one-set and rollup dimensions, one-set and rollup identifiers, ordinary and sorted summary outputs, dimension/identifier/payload expansions, and share source/output. Arrow shares are unsupported, leaving 58 executed backend cases. Local and dtplyr passed all 12 roles; Arrow passed its 10 supported roles. SQLite failed the payload expansion and both share roles, yielding #728 and #729. DuckDB failed nine roles covered by #725, #726, and #728. Focused one-set/two-set controls separated the failing paths from supported same-name cases. Both local and dtplyr also passed `.env` dimensions and identifiers through both nesting verbs, with eight verb/backend/role checks.

The same 12 roles were then repeated over 11 input shapes: data.frame, tibble, raw data.table, dtplyr, Arrow Table, RecordBatch, Dataset, Table query, Dataset query, live SQLite, and live DuckDB. Of 132 cells, 110 matched the local result, 12 failed on the same SQLite or DuckDB routes already filed, and 10 Arrow share cells were unsupported. Every supported Arrow shape passed its other 10 roles; the three local shapes and dtplyr each passed all 12.

An expression-form sweep crossed 14 forms with those 11 input shapes (154 cells). The forms included `.env` names produced by `grouping_bit()`, `grouping_id()`, `across()`, interpolation, and splicing; `.env` as a measure via `.data`; lexical `.env$offset`; a tidyselect selector; a Margin label; sorted and portable outputs; and two intentionally invalid local controls. There were 119 local-equivalent results, 22 cells with an invalid local control, and 13 differences. Two differences were the already filed DuckDB selector and sorted-output routes. The other 11 differences were reproduced by direct dbplyr/dtplyr controls or involved an Arrow expression requiring collection: direct dbplyr also fails to translate `.data[[".env"]]` on SQLite and DuckDB; a dtplyr query that captures `.env$offset` inside a function and is collected after that function returns fails in ordinary dtplyr too; direct Arrow dplyr falls back to pulling data into R for the tested `.data[[".env"]]` measure, which Margin refuses under ADR 0020's lazy-input query policy. The `across()` selection of a source `.env` column and `.grouping = .env$spec` failed the local controls, so they establish no backend comparison.

Six `.env` roles were additionally rendered with `dbplyr::tbl_lazy()` on 16 available SQL dialect simulators: Access, HANA, Hive, Impala, MariaDB, MSSQL, MySQL, ODBC, Oracle, PostgreSQL, Redshift, Snowflake, Spark SQL, SQLite, Teradata, and generic DBI. Each used an ordinary column-name control that rendered successfully. The `.env` case failed SQL translation in 92 of 96 cells with the contextual-pronoun diagnostic; only PostgreSQL share output and SQLite key, identifier, and ordinary output rendered. This is render-only evidence that the name collision reaches more translators; it does not establish that those remote databases execute or reject the generated queries. The live SQLite and DuckDB results above remain the executable evidence for #725, #726, #728, and #729.

## Limits

The randomized inputs were small and deterministic; the expression sweep sampled public syntax forms and cannot prove arbitrary R expressions free of defects. The hunt did not execute live PostgreSQL, other SQL dialects, other R versions, or other operating systems. SQL simulators validate translation only. The 13 snapshot skips mean the baseline run alone did not exercise snapshot expectations. The passing probes establish only their stated inputs and installed versions: R 4.6.1, dplyr 1.2.1, dbplyr 2.6.0, dtplyr 1.3.3, RSQLite 3.53.3, DuckDB 1.5.5, and Arrow 25.0.1.
